Demo build — not a Ministry of Health publication. This app is a demonstration under development, yet to be presented to the Ministry of Health for review, approval and collaboration. It has not been endorsed or approved by the Ministry of Health, Republic of Zambia.

The underlying guideline text is sourced from the official Zambia Standard Treatment Guidelines, Sixth Edition (2025), published by the Ministry of Health, Republic of Zambia. Reproducing that text here does not imply the Ministry's endorsement of this application.

All clinical content is sourced from the official STG document. The dataset passed source-fidelity audit, and known source caveats are marked on the affected topics.

This app is a clinical reference tool. Always verify critical dosing information against the official printed guideline.
